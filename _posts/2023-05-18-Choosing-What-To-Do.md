Are all things that are likeable to a person worth doing? We only have limited time so which ones should one choose? Is there any unique set of things one should do that is to say does there exist some magic combination one should aspire to achieve? 
Then again every person likes different things so wouldn’t everyone have individual magic combination, if there exists one. Every person also changes his taste and likes with time so that magic constant varies with time and individual, both, even when assuming there exists such an order. 
This makes a struggle of choosing them a deeply personal and dynamic(not to mention chaotic) one. We have to assess the relevance of each of our newly emerging passions as well as the ones that are fading away. 
Life thus is a constant struggle to juggle and adjust the ever changing priority list of things one likes to do.
