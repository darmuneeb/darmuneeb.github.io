I am a software developer by profession. I like to read. It is natural then to identify myself as an aspiring writer. 
I say natural as only upon reading the best authors do you come know how valuable a skill it is to be able to translate the most distilled thoughts of one's consciousness for consumption to men of varying intellects and experiences.  Moreover it is an accepted platitude that 'writing is thinking'. 
Thus I write occasionally here in the [blog](/) section.
